About NBL-2027

The 11th edition of the biennial International Conference on Nanotechnology for Better Living is organised by NIT Srinagar (J&K) in collaboration with IIT Ropar and in close association with Central University of Kashmir, SKUAST-K, AMITY New Delhi and SKD University Rajasthan, from 07–11 September 2027.

There is an option for universities, industries and organisations to be associated with the scientific event, aiming to strengthen the handshake between academia and industry, while also providing networking and career opportunities for students, scholars and aspiring industry practitioners. Dedicated panel discussions by distinguished speakers and subject-matter experts, on thematic topics relevant to the conference, shall be organised for a fruitful dialogue and exchange of ideas.

We cordially invite researchers, faculty members, students, scientists, innovators, entrepreneurs and industry professionals to participate in NBL-2027 and contribute to making the conference a meaningful platform for scientific dialogue and collaboration.

Host Institutions

National Institute of Technology Srinagar

Established in 1960 as the Regional Engineering College (REC) Srinagar, the institution was upgraded to the National Institute of Technology Srinagar in 2003. As an Institute of National Importance under the Government of India, it has emerged as a prominent centre of technical education, scientific research, innovation and human resource development.

Nestled in the picturesque Kashmir Valley, NIT Srinagar provides an inspiring environment for learning and research spanning engineering, science, technology, management and emerging interdisciplinary areas. With a growing emphasis on interdisciplinary research, innovation, entrepreneurship and collaboration, the Institute regularly organises national and international conferences and symposiums.

Indian Institute of Technology Ropar

Established in 2008, IIT Ropar is one of the newer IITs set up by the Government of India to expand and strengthen the country’s technical education and research ecosystem. It has steadily developed as a centre for quality education, advanced research, innovation and interdisciplinary collaboration.

With a growing focus on artificial intelligence, data science, advanced engineering, science and emerging technologies, IIT Ropar translates research into solutions with societal and industrial impact. Its participation in NBL-2027 reflects a shared commitment to interdisciplinary research in nanotechnology and allied sciences, including an International Symposium on Semiconductors.

Message from the General Chair

Greetings from NIT Srinagar!

The 11th edition of the biennial International Conference on “Nanotechnology for Better Living” NBL-2027 is being organised from 07–11 September 2027 — dates that mark the birth anniversary of John Dalton, who developed the atomic theory of matter.

A fusion of physical, chemical, biological, agricultural, engineering and health sciences will provide a vibrant platform to present and discuss path-breaking research ideas in nanotechnology and allied fields. The deadline for the extended abstract submission (one page with a schematic diagram / graphical abstract / sketch and conclusion) is 30th March 2027.

I look forward to your kind cooperation in forwarding this information to your colleagues, students and friends for wide circulation, and to your participation in this sacred place of saints from 07–11 September 2027.

Dr. M. A. ShahGeneral Chair, NBL Series at NIT Srinagar
