Abstract Submission Guidelines

Before you upload your abstract

Your one-page abstract must be original work and free from any kind of plagiarism.

  • Submit an extended one-page abstract with a schematic diagram / graphical abstract / sketch and conclusion.
  • The abstract should be around 400–500 words, written as a single paragraph covering the aim, scope and conclusion.
  • State briefly the purpose, results and major conclusions.
  • Avoid references and uncommon abbreviations; the abstract should stand alone, separate from the article.
  • All abstracts are reviewed and checked for plagiarism / AI-generated content.
  • Follow the prescribed format before uploading.

Poster-Walkway of Discovery & Innovation

A unique opportunity for scientists and researchers to showcase their innovations and research in the field of nanoscience. This platform is designed to encourage young researchers and entrepreneurs to present their research and innovative ideas to a wider audience. The event is expected to attract potential investors, heads of leading R&D laboratories, venture capitalists and industry leaders. The best posters will be suitably rewarded.

Research-Industry Meet

A unique platform connecting young researchers and entrepreneurs with industry leaders to showcase research, innovations and ideas with potential for real-world applications. The meet brings together potential investors, heads of leading R&D laboratories, venture capitalists and industry leaders, fostering opportunities for collaboration, technology transfer and innovation.
