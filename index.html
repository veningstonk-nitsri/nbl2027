Welcome to NBL-2027

The 11th edition of the biennial International Conference on Nanotechnology for Better Living (NBL-2027) is being organised by NIT Srinagar (J&K) in collaboration with IIT Ropar, and in close association with Central University of Kashmir, SKUAST-K, AMITY New Delhi and SKD University Rajasthan, from 07–11 September 2027.

The dates mark the birth anniversary of John Dalton — the British chemist and physicist who developed the atomic theory of matter. A fabulous fusion of physical, chemical, biological, agricultural, engineering and health sciences will provide a vibrant platform to present and discuss path-breaking research ideas in nanotechnology and allied fields, and serve as an interface between academia and industry. A galaxy of local innovators and entrepreneurs shall be a part of the summit, along with students and scholars.

The event is organised in the backdrop of the NBL series, held since 2011 as memorable joint events between IIT Kanpur and NIT Srinagar in association with many prestigious universities. In this 11th edition, dedicated sessions titled “Nurturing minds for a better world” shall be conducted by a few stalwarts, handing this multidisciplinary science over to our young and smart generation.

NBL Spectrum

The scientific programme of NBL-2027 will feature:

  • Plenary lectures by internationally renowned scientists
  • Keynote talks by experts from academia and industry
  • Emerging trends in nanotechnology and advanced materials
  • Workshops, panel discussions and interactive sessions with stalwarts
  • Oral and poster presentations by researchers and young scientists
  • Industry-focused discussions on commercialisation and future technologies
  • Networking opportunities for collaborative research and innovation
  • Exhibition of globally recognised products, equipment, instruments and tools
  • Special sessions for Women in Science, Technology and Innovation
  • Young Researcher, Young Scientist and Best Poster Awards
  • Special sessions by DRDO / CSIR / DAE / MoJS / MNRE
  • A special session for local innovators and entrepreneurs
